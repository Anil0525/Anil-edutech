Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Plans (One-time payments)

Premium plans are one-time payments. There is no auto-renewal and no auto-debit. Monthly = 30 days, Yearly = 365 days, Trial (₹1) = 1 day, counted from the time of activation. Premium మీరు మళ్ళీ కొనుగోలు చేస్తేనే కొనసాగుతుంది.

2. When you get a refund

We refund the full amount in these cases:

  • Payment was deducted but Premium was not activated within 24 hours.
  • You were charged twice for the same plan.
  • A technical problem on our side stopped you from using Premium, and we could not fix it within 3 days.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI / card / net banking) within 5–7 working days.

3. When refunds are not given

Once Premium is activated and working, change of mind or not attempting the tests is not a reason for refund. The ₹1 trial is non-refundable.

4. Cancellation

Because there is no auto-renewal, there is nothing to cancel. Your Premium simply ends on the expiry date.

5. How to request a refund / get help

Message us on WhatsApp with your registered Gmail and payment ID / UTR (screenshot is fine). We reply within 1–2 working days.
